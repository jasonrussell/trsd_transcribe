SUMMARY OF BOARD OF SELECTMEN MEETING - AUGUST 13, 2026
---
This transcript covers discussions and reports from a Board of Selectmen meeting, primarily focusing on student transitions, facility updates, and an in-depth review of the Fiscal Year 2026 budget.

I. Key Discussion Points:

*   **Student Transition Programming:** The board discussed programs designed to ease middle school transitions (Grade 5 to Grade 6), emphasizing the value of summer tours led by older students (7th graders) for both students and parents.
*   **Facilities & Infrastructure:** Updates covered facility improvements, including HVAC system upgrades at Pollard Elementary School, which were reported as successful. Discussions also touched on deferred maintenance issues across the district, such as athletic wing renovations and building structural concerns (e.g., belfry).

II. Fiscal Year 2026 Budget Summary:
The board received a detailed presentation of the FY26 budget, highlighting major budgetary reductions ($6.6 million delta between proposed and voted budget) in several areas:
*   **Personnel:** 37 positions were eliminated (10 via Reduction In Force/RIF).
*   **Capital Projects:** Two CIP projects were approved for FY26 (Atkinson Academy fencing and belfry); others were deferred.
*   **Program Cuts:** Significant cuts were made to instructional resources, technology, athletic equipment, and field trip funding.

III. Policy & Educational Topics:
A prolonged discussion occurred regarding the appropriateness of displaying political or ideological symbols (flags) in school classrooms. Consensus was difficult due to differing views on viewpoint neutrality versus educational focus, leading to a request for policy review. Further discussions covered:
*   **Special Education:** Concerns were raised about the significant percentage of tax dollars dedicated to Special Education services and the need for better transparency regarding service provision and caseload sizes.

IV. Financial Review & Unassigned Fund Balance (UFB):
The Treasurer presented data on the UFB, explaining that it serves as a crucial cash flow management tool, allowing the district flexibility when local town funding payments are delayed or insufficient. The board acknowledged this fund's historical use but was cautioned about its current size relative to annual operating costs.

V. Action Items & Next Steps:
1.  **Budget Meeting Calendar:** A joint meeting date (Sept 24th) and subsequent budget review dates were established, aiming for the Board and Budget Committee to align on priorities before presenting a final plan.
2.  **DELIBERATIVE SESSION DATE:** Motion passed to move the Timberlane Regional School District Deliberative Session to Saturday, February 6, 2027.
