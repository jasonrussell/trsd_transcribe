SUMMARY OF THE TIMBERLANE REGIONAL SCHOOL DISTRICT BUDGET COMMITTEE MEETING - SEPTEMBER 10TH, 2026

I. PROCEDURAL MATTERS AND GOVERNANCE
- **Minutes Approval:** The committee voted to accept the minutes from the March 26th organizational meeting and the subsequent regular meeting of 2026.
- **Committee Size:** The committee confirmed its membership, with one member (Julia) excused for lateness.

II. ADMINISTRATIVE AND BOARD UPDATES
- **Meeting Schedule Change:** School Board meetings will begin at 6:30 PM, effective next September board meeting.
- **Deliberative Session Date:** The deliberative session date for 2027 has been determined to be Saturday, February 6th (the second Saturday in the calendar year).
- **Fund Balance:** The School Board voted to retain $500,000 from the unassigned fund balance for emergency use.
- **Enrollment Statistics:** As of June 2026, the district had 3,187 enrolled students and 591 employees.

III. FINANCIAL AND BUDGETARY REVIEW (FY25 vs FY26)
- **Financial Health:** The dashboard review showed that for both revenue and expenses in fiscal year 2026, the collection/spending was at approximately 100% of expected budget amounts.
- **Expenditure Breakdown:** Expenses are primarily broken down into operating costs, salaries, and benefits.
- **Special Education Costs:** A major discussion point highlighted that due to staff shortages over the last few years (two to three years), many special education positions must be filled by highly costly contractors ($30-$40+ per hour for paraeducators). This results in significant financial impact compared to internal staffing salaries.
- **Facility Improvements (CIP):** Significant work was completed in FY26, including 62,000 feet of insulated roofing and 11 new energy recovery units, largely at the Pollard school.

IV. KEY LEGISLATIVE AND BUDGETARY DISCUSSIONS
1.  **Challenge to HB 1300:** A court motion was filed challenging House Bill 1300 (which set district/tax caps), arguing that the language is confusing, misleading, and improperly bundles options for voters. This issue will be monitored.
2.  **Default Budget Authority Shift:** Critically, it was stated that the statutory obligation to put forward the default budget has shifted from the School Board directly to the Budget Committee (this group).
3.  **Cyberbullying Policy Changes:** New state law changes regarding cyber bullying and student policy will mandate statutory updates by January 1, 2027, requiring increased community engagement.
4.  **Workshops Suggested:** Multiple attendees advocated for a dedicated workshop or meeting to help the committee understand how the default budget is calculated, given the change in statutory responsibility.

V. OUTSTANDING ITEMS
- **Next Meeting Focus:** The next scheduled joint meeting will focus on defining both the Budget Committee's and School Board's respective priorities.
