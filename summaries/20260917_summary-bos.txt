SUMMARY OF 2026-09-17 SCHOOL BOARD MEETING MINUTES
===================================================
This transcript records a comprehensive school board meeting covering administrative approvals, curriculum updates, financial reviews, facility reports, and significant policy/transparency discussions.

I. ADMINISTRATIVE BUSINESS & GOVERNANCE
- Minutes for Sept. 3rd (Public and Non-public) were approved unanimously.
- Board members raised serious concerns regarding the lack of transparency and inappropriate conduct observed during prior deliberations, demanding accountability from administrators and board members moving forward.

II. CURRICULUM REVISIONS (FIRST READS APPROVED)
The following curriculum changes were reviewed and approved for first read:
1. English for Trades: A new half-credit course focused on practical writing skills essential for students entering trades.
2. New Hampshire History: Curriculum documents supporting a state history course mandated by law.
3. Algebra 2: Standardization of curriculum documents to ensure consistent content regardless of whether the student took the year-long or split version of the course.

III. FINANCIAL AND BUDGET REPORTING
- The board reviewed an "all funds" budget model, expanding beyond just the general fund to include sources like food service and grants.
- Key financial takeaways included total projected revenue (approx. $19.2 million) and a commitment to maintaining budgetary stability despite varying funding streams.

IV. FACILITIES AND INFRASTRUCTURE REPORTING
- A detailed summary of summer work was provided, noting major capital improvements across the district:
    *   Roofing and insulation replacements (over 62,000 sq ft total).
    *   Installation/replacement of Energy Recovery Units (ERUs) and HVAC components.
    *   Electrical service upgrades to support new equipment.
- The board discussed utilizing advanced Building Management Systems (BMS) for load balancing and efficiency, ensuring that facility spending yields measurable cost reductions.

V. KEY POLICY & TRANSPARENCY ISSUES
- Right to Know Concerns: A primary point of contention was the transparency of fund usage, particularly regarding discrepancies between reported funds spent versus stated budgetary status, suggesting potential gaps in financial reporting.
- Policy Adoption: Policies were reviewed for first read approval, including updated rules on bullying prevention and adopting a Leave for Uniformed Service Members.
- Legislative Updates: The board discussed the impact of state legislation (e.g., tax caps) that will require future action and planning with the Budget Committee.

VI. MAJOR PROJECTS & RECOMMENDATIONS
*   Overnight Field Trip: A proposal was made to conduct an environmental research trip in Panama in 2027, which was approved for first read consideration.
*   Future Planning: Discussions included consolidating facility management into a single emergency operating plan across the district.
